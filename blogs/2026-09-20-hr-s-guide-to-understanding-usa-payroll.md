Understanding USA payroll is one of the most critical competencies for any HR professional. Payroll represents the largest single operating cost for most organizations, and getting it wrong triggers IRS penalties, state fines, and employee trust issues. This guide gives HR teams a clear, compliance-ready breakdown of how US payroll works in 2026.
